This report documents the virtual internship completed at Thiranex, an MSME-registered Educational Technology (EdTech) partnership enterprise headquartered in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India (www.thiranex.in). Founded in 2025 under the corporate leadership of Hariharan M (CEO), Jaigher Daniel (COO), and Kevin Beryl (CTO), the organization operates with the core mission of democratizing technical education by providing free, accessible virtual internships across more than 15 professional domains. The program successfully bridged academic theory and modern industry practice through self-paced learning, remote professional mentorship, and hands-on project execution. By removing geographic barriers for students, Thiranex provides verified training certificates, instant offer documentation, and structured portfolio development designed to enhance graduate employability and build engineering-ready technical competencies.
